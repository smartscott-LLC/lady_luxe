The rain tapped a soft, steady rhythm against the tall apartment windows, blurring the city lights below into streaks of amber and gold. Inside, the air was warm, scented faintly with vanilla, bergamot, and the lingering smoke of a freshly lit candle.

Elena stood before the gilded mirror, taking her time. There was no rush tonight. That was the secret to true luxury, she had learned—never hurrying the ritual.

She reached for the black satin box resting on her vanity and lifted the tissue paper. Inside lay her latest indulgence from **Lady Luxe**: an exquisite midnight-black ensemble by **Roza**, woven with French-inspired lace that caught the candlelight like spun silk. As she fastened the delicate clasps, her posture shifted instinctively. Shoulders back, chin lifted, a quiet smile curving her lips.

She didn't wear pieces like this merely to be seen; she wore them for the way they made the room feel entirely hers.

Over the lace, she draped a floor-length silk robe, leaving it loosely tied at the waist, and slipped into a pair of satin heels. On the turntable in the living room, a velvet-voiced ballad drifted through the hallway. Two crystal coupes sat waiting on the marble coffee table beside a chilled bottle of rosé.

When the soft knock finally echoed at the door, Elena paused for just a heartbeat, savoring the anticipation. She smoothed the silk over her hip, crossed the room, and turned the brass handle.

The night was just beginning—and she was dressed for every unforgettable moment of it.
